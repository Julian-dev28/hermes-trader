How to read this page

The What to do card turns everything below into instructions with a trigger and a stop. The rest is the evidence behind it.

Trend shape is slope + R² + efficiency, not just a return — a coin up 20% on a round trip is labelled CHOP, and the label is what ranks it.

Forecasts are a shrunken-drift baseline, and every lane ships the backtest that says what it is worth. Where that backtest says "coin flip", the tab says so in amber rather than hiding it.

The AI pass is optional: it reads these numbers, it never produces one.
